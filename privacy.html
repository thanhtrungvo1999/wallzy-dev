Privacy Policy

Learn how Wallzy handles information when you use the service.

1. Information we process

Account information. If you sign in with Google, Wallzy uses information provided by Google authentication, such as your name, email address, profile photo, and account identifier, to create and maintain your Wallzy account.

Saved content. When you are signed in, Wallzy stores information needed for features such as your favorite wallpapers and saved gradients.

Wallpaper content. Public wallpapers displayed by Wallzy may include image URLs, titles, categories, keywords, and timestamps needed to display and organize the wallpaper library.

2. How we use information

Wallzy may use processed information to provide and maintain features, authenticate accounts, associate saved content with users, display and organize wallpaper content, maintain security and performance, and improve the service where applicable.

3. Third-party services

Wallzy uses third-party services to operate parts of the service, including Google/Firebase for authentication and database services. Wallzy may also load external resources, image delivery services, advertising services, or analytics technologies. Those providers may process information according to their own policies.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Wallzy or its third-party providers may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to support authentication, preferences, security, advertising, and service functionality.

5. Data retention and deletion

Wallzy may retain account-related information and saved content for as long as needed to provide associated features or for legitimate operational purposes. If you want account-related data or saved content deleted, contact us through the contact method provided on the website.

6. Children's privacy

Wallzy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Security

Wallzy uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information processed by the service. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The current version is published on this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request deletion of account-related data, please use the contact method provided on the Wallzy website.
